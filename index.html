Executive Summary

Two Chairs ran an always-on therapist recruitment campaign across Google Search, Meta (Facebook/Instagram), and LinkedIn in May 2026, targeting licensed therapists in California and Washington. Total investment of $1,606.66 generated 303K impressions and 3,826 clicks at a blended CPC of $0.42. The campaign recorded 7 total conversions — 6 on LinkedIn and 1 on Meta. Going into June, we have slowed Meta spend to reassess strategy and will be optimising towards Landing Page Views, while increasing LinkedIn investment given its stronger conversion performance. Google Search will be closely monitored with an A/B test of exact match keywords and a Performance Max campaign launching in June.

Key Insights & Opportunities
01
LinkedIn Leads on Conversions — Budget Shifting Up
LinkedIn delivered 6 of the 7 total conversions at a 1.89% CTR — the highest engagement rate across all platforms. Given this performance, LinkedIn budget will be increased in June to capitalise on its proven conversion efficiency with the therapist audience.
02
Meta — Slowing Down to Reassess Strategy
Meta reached 134K unique profiles at a low $2.87 CPM but returned only 1 conversion. Spend was intentionally slowed towards the end of May to reassess approach. Going forward, Meta campaigns will be optimised towards Landing Page Views to drive more qualified traffic before scaling spend again.
03
Google Search — Monitoring & Testing in June
Launched May 25 with a strong 3.58% CTR but still in its learning phase. Google will be closely monitored in June. An A/B test of exact match keywords will run alongside a new Performance Max campaign to explore automated placements and incremental reach.
04
May 29–31 Meta Delivery Drop — Pacing Risk
Meta spend fell from ~$62/day to under $5/day in the final 3 days of May, indicating end-of-month budget pacing. With Meta spend decreasing in June, even pacing should still be maintained to avoid wasted delivery at either end of the flight.
June Direction — All Channels
What We're Doing in June
  • Meta — Slowing down to reassess. Spend was intentionally pulled back in late May. Going forward, campaigns will optimise towards Landing Page Views to better qualify traffic before scaling up again.
  • LinkedIn — Increasing budget. Strongest conversion performer with 6 conversions and best-in-class CTR. Scaling investment in June to drive more therapist applications.
  • Google Search — Close monitoring. Campaign still in learning phase with only 7 days of data. Will be observed closely without major structural interference to allow Smart Bidding to stabilise.
  • Google — A/B test: Exact Match keywords. Testing exact match alongside current match types to assess whether tighter keyword control improves CPC and conversion quality in the mental health space.
  • Google — Performance Max campaign launch. Introducing a PMax campaign in June to leverage automated delivery across Search, Display, YouTube, and Discover for incremental therapist reach.
